# 🛒 Supermarket Transaction & Retail Performance Analytics

## 🌟 Project Overview
An advanced retail sales and financial transaction analytics framework designed to process multi-branch supermarket operations (covering branches in Yangon, Naypyitaw, and Mandalay). This project harnesses the power of Python and Pandas to evaluate revenue streams, product line profitability, customer purchasing behaviors, and transaction trends, delivering data-driven insights for retail store management.

## 📊 Key Analytical Modules & Findings
- **Revenue & Basket Analysis:** Processed comprehensive transaction records, tracking a total revenue baseline of **$322,966** across 1,000 unique customer invoices with an average basket size of **$322.97**.
- **Branch Performance Comparison:** Evaluated sales distribution across branches (Giza, Alex, and Cairo), highlighting top-performing locations and transaction volumes.
- **Product Line Profitability:** Analyzed sales across multiple product categories (Food & Beverages, Sports & Travel, Electronic Accessories, Fashion, and Home Lifestyle) to isolate high-demand items.
- **Customer Segmentation & Payment Trends:** Assessed member vs. normal customer purchasing patterns alongside preferred payment methods (E-wallet, Cash, and Credit Cards).
- **Temporal & Daily Trends:** Examined peak shopping hours, day-of-the-week transaction density, and monthly sales trajectories (January to March fluctuations).

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Libraries
- **Language:** Python
- **Data Manipulation & Analysis:** Pandas, NumPy
- **Data Visualization:** Matplotlib, Seaborn

## 💡 Business Impact & Strategic Value
This analytical pipeline equips retail executives with clear operational transparency—identifying peak sales windows, top-performing product categories, and regional sales drivers to optimize inventory management, promotional targeting, and overall profitability.
Retail transaction analytics and sales performance dashboard using Pandas, NumPy, and exploratory data visualization.
